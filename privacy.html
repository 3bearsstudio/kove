This Privacy Policy explains how 3 Bears Studio LLC collects, uses, discloses, and protects information in connection with the KOVE mobile application and describes the choices and rights available to you. It is built on a deliberate principle: the information you would most want kept private is designed never to reach us in the first place. By downloading, accessing, or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. 4. Information that stays on your device — we never receive it

The core of KOVE runs entirely on your device and syncs, when you choose, through your own private iCloud account (Apple CloudKit) under your Apple ID. We do not collect, receive, store, transmit to ourselves, or have any ability to read the following:

  • Your blocks — which apps and categories you block, your schedules, unlock methods, and settings.
  • Your habits and their complete history.
  • Your focus and mindfulness sessions, streaks, and statistics.
  • Your saved places used for location‑based blocking.
  • Your Screen Time / device‑usage data shown in the App's Insights view.

This is not merely a promise; it is how the App is built. This data is written only to your device and to your private iCloud, which is controlled by your Apple ID and governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. Under the definition used by Apple and by U.S. privacy statutes, information that is never transmitted off your device in a way that lets us or our partners access it is not "collected," which is why none of it appears in our collection disclosures below.

Screen Time specifically. Apple's Screen Time framework is designed so that usage data can only be displayed inside a sandboxed, on‑device report. The App itself cannot read the raw numbers, and none of that data is transmitted to us. Any categorization of apps you set for the Insights view is stored on your device and, where applicable, in your private iCloud.

Location specifically. If you use location‑based blocking, your device evaluates the geofence locally using Apple's Core Location framework. Your location and your saved places are not sent to us; they remain on your device and in your private iCloud. KOVE collects no location data of any kind. We do not maintain location‑history logs, and our analytics provider's IP‑based geolocation feature has been disabled (Section 5).

Apple Health specifically. If you connect Apple Health, KOVE reads metrics such as step count, active minutes, distance, or sleep on your device to power habit tracking and, if you choose, health challenges. These raw readings are processed locally and are not transmitted to us. The single, narrow exception — one aggregated daily number submitted only when you join a health challenge in the optional social features — is described in Section 5.3.

To remove this device/iCloud data, delete the App, or remove KOVE's data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Because it is your data in your iCloud, its deletion is within your control and independent of us.

5. Information we collect

We collect only the categories of information described in this section. We have organized them by the technical system that receives them.

5.1 Anonymous product analytics (PostHog)

To understand how KOVE is used in aggregate and to improve it, we collect anonymous product analytics using PostHog, processed in the European Union.

  • The analytics are anonymous. We do not attach your name, email address, or account to them, and we never call any function that would associate analytics events with your identity. Each installation is represented by a stable, anonymous identifier that contains no personal information; it lets us count returning users accurately without knowing who you are. We do not attempt to re‑identify you from analytics.
  • Events record counts, durations grouped into ranges, and fixed category labels — for example, that "a focus session ended," "a block was created," or "a permission was granted." The events are engineered so that they cannot carry the content of what you block, the names or details of your habits, anything you type, or your raw screen‑time numbers. This restriction is enforced in the App's source code by the type of data an analytics event is allowed to carry, not merely by policy.
  • Basic technical context that the analytics software records with each event may include your device model, operating‑system version, App version, timezone/locale, and a coarse timestamp. Your IP address is transmitted to the analytics provider as an unavoidable part of any internet request; we have configured the provider not to derive your geographic location from it, and the provider is instructed to discard it after processing.
  • We do not use session recording, screen recording, keystroke logging, heatmaps, or "autocapture." Only the specific, named events described above are collected.

On the Apple App Store privacy label these correspond to Product Interaction, Other Usage Data, and the anonymous installation identifier (Device ID) — all declared not linked to you and not used to track you.

5.2 Diagnostic data

To find and fix problems, we collect limited diagnostic data through the same anonymous analytics system. When an error occurs, we may record a technical error domain and numeric code — never the error's human‑readable message, which can contain incidental content. Diagnostic data is anonymous and corresponds to Other Diagnostic Data on the App Store privacy label, not linked to you and not used to track you.

5.3 Optional social features (Firebase)

KOVE includes optional social and accountability features — friends, circles, challenges, activity feed, and accountability partners. These features are entirely opt‑in. If you never sign in to the social features, none of the information in this Section 5.3 is collected, and you can use the entire core of KOVE — focus, blocking, and habits — without an account.

If you choose to sign in (using Sign in with Apple or Google Sign‑In), we use Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, and Firebase Cloud Messaging) to operate these features, and we collect:

  • Account identifier. An anonymous user ID (assigned by Firebase) that keys your friend connections, challenges, and circles.
  • Sign‑in email address. Held by the sign‑in provider as part of your credential. With Sign in with Apple, this is typically an Apple private‑relay address rather than your real email, and the App itself does not display or store it. We use it only as part of authentication.
  • Display name. The name you choose to show to friends and circle members. It is length‑limited and sanitized.
  • Content you deliberately share with people you connect to. Your participation and results in shared challenges and circles, and system‑generated activity events (for example, "completed a focus session," or — for accountability partners you specifically opt into — "took a break from a block" or "a streak ended"). These activity events are generated from controlled, predefined labels; they do not carry free text that you type.
  • Fitness totals — only if you join a health challenge. To place you on a challenge leaderboard, the App reads a metric on your device (such as your daily step count, active minutes, or hours slept) and submits a single aggregated daily total for that challenge. Raw Apple Health samples never leave your device. If you join no health challenge, no fitness data is submitted.
  • Notification token. If you enable notifications, we store a device push token so we can deliver accountability and challenge notifications to you.

This information is used only to operate the social features you chose to use. On the App Store privacy label these appear as linked to you (they are tied to your account) but not used to track you. The corresponding Apple data categories are Email Address, Name, User ID, and Fitness.

5.4 Information collected automatically by supporting services

When your device communicates with the supporting services above, standard technical information — such as your IP address and the timing of requests — is transmitted as an inherent part of any internet connection and is processed by our service providers (Apple, Google/Firebase, and PostHog) to route traffic, secure the Services, and prevent abuse. As described in Section 5.1, we have configured our analytics provider not to use IP for geolocation. We do not use this technical information to build advertising profiles or to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

5.5 Information we do not collect

For clarity, KOVE does not collect: your precise or approximate location; your contacts; your photos or camera roll; your microphone or audio; your health records beyond the single challenge total described in 5.3; the content of your habits, blocks, notes, or focus sessions; your browsing history; or any information used for advertising or cross‑app tracking.
